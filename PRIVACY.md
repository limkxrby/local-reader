# Local Reader privacy policy

Applies to version 1.0.3.

Local Reader stores article text, URLs, titles, dates, tags, reading state, notes, highlights and trash inside your browser profile using IndexedDB. No account is required. There is no analytics, advertising, telemetry, cloud database, or sync service.

The toolbar reads a page only when you ask to save it. Pocket CSV files and extracted Omnivore folders are processed locally and never uploaded. Explicit batch downloads contact the websites you selected after requesting optional site access. Those sites see your network request and IP address. Downloads omit sign-in cookies and referrers. Following an original-page link visits that site using your normal browser settings.

Saved reader pages strip scripts and remote media. The database and JSON exports are not encrypted by Local Reader. Anyone with access to your browser profile or backups may be able to read them.

Trash is recoverable: trashed articles remain in storage and backups until the extension/profile is removed. Uninstalling the extension or deleting your browser profile may erase the library. Backups remain wherever you saved them; you control their retention and deletion.

Revoke optional site access through your browser's extension settings. Core saving, reading, import, search and export work without persistent access to every site.

Questions: [project issues](https://github.com/limkxrby/local-reader/issues). Reports there are public. Do not attach private articles, browsing history or backups.
