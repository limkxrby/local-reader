Local Reader privacy

Local Reader stores saved article text, URLs, titles, dates, tags, reading state, notes, highlights, and trash in IndexedDB inside your browser profile. No account is required. This version has no analytics, advertising, telemetry, cloud database, or sync service.

Clicking the toolbar reads the page you chose. CSV files and extracted Omnivore folders are processed on your device. Imported files are not uploaded. Explicit batch downloads contact the websites you selected and require optional site access. Those websites see the network request and your IP address; requests omit sign-in cookies and referrers. Clicking original-page links visits the corresponding site under your normal browser settings.

Saved reader pages strip scripts, remote media and resource-loading attributes. Local Reader does not encrypt its database or JSON exports. Anyone with access to your browser profile or backup files may be able to read them.

Move to trash hides an item; restore it from the Trash view. Items in trash remain in storage and exported backups. Uninstalling the extension or deleting the browser profile may erase the entire library. Export regular backups using the library controls; keep them secure and delete unwanted backup files yourself.

Optional site access can be revoked through your browser's extension settings. Core saving, reading, import, search, and export do not require granting access to every website.

Privacy questions and issue reports: Local Reader project issues. This is a public service; do not include private articles, browsing history, or backup files in a public report.

Applies to version 1.0.4.
